## The Brief

Paid media could help the restaurant reach customers already searching for pizza, restaurants,
delivery, takeaway, or a place to eat in Bharatpur–Chitwan. It could also waste budget quickly if
traffic was sent to weak destinations, tracking counted clicks instead of outcomes, or restaurant
capacity could not fulfil the demand created.

The brief was to determine which channels deserved attention, how a first campaign should be
structured, what had to be fixed before launch, and which paid options were premature.

<div class="cs-accordion not-prose">

<details open>
<summary>Methodology</summary>
<div class="cs-panel">

- **Readiness assessment**: evaluated customer pathways, landing destinations, offer clarity,
  service boundaries, tracking, account ownership, response capacity, and data quality
- **Intent and audience design**: mapped customer occasions and search behaviour to brand,
  pizza, restaurant, delivery, takeaway, and location themes
- **Campaign architecture**: defined platform roles, account structure, match types, negatives,
  geography, scheduling, devices, messaging, and Google Ads assets
- **Conversion and budget governance**: separated primary business outcomes from diagnostic
  events and established test, review, pause, and scale rules
- **Channel sequencing**: assessed Search, Meta, TikTok paid, Performance Max, Display,
  remarketing, YouTube, brand, and competitor campaigns by readiness and strategic fit

</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>What was delivered</summary>
<div class="cs-panel">

- An executive paid-media strategy and readiness assessment
- Paid-media objectives, audience roles, customer-journey contribution, and platform priorities
- Recommended campaign portfolio, Google Ads account structure, keyword and match-type approach,
  negative-keyword logic, geography, schedule, and device considerations
- Message framework, responsive Search ad examples, and asset recommendations
- Clear positions on Brand Search, competitor terms, Performance Max, Display, remarketing,
  YouTube, and paid social
- Landing-page and offer requirements, budget-allocation principles, bidding approach, conversion
  tracking, attribution, KPI, reporting, optimisation, governance, and launch controls

</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>The result</summary>
<div class="cs-panel">

- **Google Search became the first paid priority** because it can capture customers expressing
  immediate local restaurant and pizza intent
- **The launch scope was deliberately narrow**: appropriate brand protection and one controlled
  pizza-intent campaign before any broad expansion
- **Meta was positioned as secondary and conditional**, while TikTok paid, Performance Max,
  Display, YouTube, and competitor campaigns were deferred pending better evidence
- **Paid media gained enforceable gates** around pages, tracking, customer outcomes, policy,
  budget, and service capacity — preventing a launch driven by channel enthusiasm alone

</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Reflection</summary>
<div class="cs-panel">

The strategic value of paid media was speed, not certainty. Search could expose the business to
high-intent demand quickly, but it could not repair a fragmented journey or prove a completed
order without operational data.

The most responsible recommendation was therefore conditional. Build the destination and
measurement first, launch a small test, inspect search terms and confirmed customer quality, and
scale only where the evidence supports it.
