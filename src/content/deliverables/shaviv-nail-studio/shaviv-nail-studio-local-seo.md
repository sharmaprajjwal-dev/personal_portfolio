## The Brief

Shaviv Nail Studio & Training Center had no claimed presence on Google Search or Maps —
meaning every local search for a nail studio or beauty academy in Bharatpur was sending
demand to competitors by default. The brief: claim and build out a Google Business Profile
from zero, and get it converting local search intent into real bookings, fast — not a
slow-burn six-month SEO project.

<div class="cs-accordion not-prose">

<details open>
<summary>Methodology</summary>
<div class="cs-panel">

- **Profile foundation**: claimed and verified the listing, then built out categories,
  service list, business hours, and photos to fully match how the business actually operates
  — the details that decide whether Google surfaces a listing at all before a single review
  exists
- **Keyword-aligned description**: wrote the business description and service categories
  around the actual local search terms a prospective client would use, not generic
  industry language
- **Google Posts cadence**: used Google's native Posts feature to keep the profile active
  and keyword-relevant between review cycles, rather than leaving it static after setup
- **Review generation**: built a simple ask-at-the-right-moment flow for both studio clients
  and training academy students, so genuine reviews accumulated from day one instead of
  trickling in organically over months

</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>What was delivered</summary>
<div class="cs-panel">

- A fully claimed, verified, and completed Google Business Profile — categories, services,
  hours, photos, and a keyword-aligned description
- A recurring Google Posts cadence to keep the listing active and locally relevant
- A lightweight review-generation flow that took the listing from zero to 15 reviews at a
  4.3★ average inside four weeks
- A profile structured to keep compounding — every mechanism set up (posts, categories,
  review flow) is designed to keep working after the initial engagement, not just for the
  first month

</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>The result</summary>
<div class="cs-panel">

- **From nothing to a live, discoverable listing** in the same window most local SEO
  engagements spend just getting the account claimed and verified
- **15 reviews at a 4.3★ average within four weeks** of a brand-new, unclaimed listing —
  see the live profile: <a href="https://maps.app.goo.gl/2poN8aW7fViniA8Y9" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Shaviv Nail Studio &amp; Training Center on Google Maps</a>
- **Built to last, not just to launch** — the categories, posting cadence, and review flow
  are structural, so the listing keeps earning visibility after the engagement ends rather
  than needing constant hands-on management

</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Reflection</summary>
<div class="cs-panel">

Local SEO for a small, founder-run business doesn't need to be complicated to be effective —
it needs to be complete and started early. Most of the visibility gap here wasn't a ranking
problem, it was a claiming-and-completeness problem: a fully built-out profile with real,
recent reviews will consistently outperform a half-filled one, regardless of how sophisticated
the rest of the strategy is. The fastest win available to a business like this is almost
always the one sitting unclaimed on Google.

</div>
</details>
